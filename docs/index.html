The Business Flow

Internal YouTube Publishing Automation

The Business Flow is an internal content production and publishing system used to create, upload, manage, and schedule original business documentary videos for the official The Business Flow YouTube channel.

How YouTube API Services are used

  • Authenticate the authorized The Business Flow YouTube channel.
  • Upload original video files and thumbnails.
  • Read basic channel and uploaded-video metadata for validation.
  • Schedule future publication of uploaded videos.

This tool is not offered as a public consumer application. It is operated by the channel owner for internal publishing workflows.
